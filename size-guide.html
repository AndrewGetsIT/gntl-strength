Custom pants and boots are cut to your own measurements, not a size chart. Use the guide below to take yours before ordering.

How to measure

Chest

Wrap the tape under your arms, across the widest part of your chest. Keep the tape level and snug, not tight.

Waist

Measure around your natural waistline, where your torso bends side to side. Keep one finger of slack.

Hip

Measure around the fullest part of your hips, roughly 8 inches below your waist.

Length

For pants, measure from your waistband to where you want the hem to fall. For jackets, from the base of the collar to the hem.
